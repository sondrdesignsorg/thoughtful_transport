About us

A local family, driving for your family

Thoughtful Transport LLC is a locally owned, family run company providing non-emergency, wheelchair-accessible transportation across the island of Oahu.

Thoughtful Transport is newly started, with a clear purpose: to help kupuna and anyone who needs extra accommodation get to their destinations and appointments safely and on time.

We want our customers to enjoy their life and their independence, and reliable transportation is a big part of that. We understand this community because it is ours, and we strive to provide the service and care your loved ones and ohana deserve.

Every ride is in a fully equipped wheelchair-accessible van. Tyson, our operations manager and driver, is known for patient, compassionate service every step of the way.

Services

Wheelchair-accessible rides for wherever the day takes you

Scheduled, non-emergency transportation across Oahu in a fully equipped wheelchair-accessible vehicle, with a driver committed to a smooth, comfortable ride.

Medical appointments

Doctor visits, specialists, imaging, therapy and follow-ups — we build in time so you arrive unhurried and we wait or return as you prefer.

Dialysis & recurring care

Standing weekly schedules with the same familiar driver, so treatment days become one less thing to arrange.

Hospital & care-home discharge

Getting home, or moving between facilities, with careful handling and a calm pace at both ends of the trip.

Airport arrivals & departures

Curb-to-terminal help with luggage and timing for visiting family, at Daniel K. Inouye International and inter-island gates.

Errands, church & family time

Groceries, the salon, Sunday service, a birthday party at the beach park. Life is not only appointments.
